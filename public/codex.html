OMEGA

FLOWSTATE SPACE COMBAT  ·  LOCAL REFERENCE

Neon vector arena on a 16000 × 16000 torus. Hub at center. Cardinal castles sit 5120 out. Camera zoom 0.56. Steam / desktop is the goal; phone is optional. Career XP is local for now; Steam Cloud is planned later.

Numbers below are Easy unless noted. Medium / Hard raise HP, spawn rates, and pressure. Extra life stays every 10,000 score.

THE LOOP

  1. Title → NEW GAME or a save slot → start any unlocked assault wave.
  2. Fly, mine, fight. Dock at the Hub to upgrade and cash kill bounties.
  3. Assault waves are timed combat. Between them is a rest.
  4. Die with lives left → respawn invulnerable (2.1s). Lives hit 0 → Continue.
  5. Score is this run. XP is career and survives Continue and new runs. No XP leaderboard.

Continue resets Score to 0 and the extra-life threshold. Ship, loadout, Points, bounty, and assault wave stay. RESET XP also resets ship locks and the current hull to DSF 22.

You are safe overlapping the Hub even if you are not docked. H / HOME flies all the way in and docks. Warp is 2560 in 1s, 2.4s cool. Mid-warp, S / reverse / stick back dumps you where you are. Cooldown still runs.

BLACK HOLE BOMB — GATED

Built, then turned off. Set blackHole.enabled to bring it back. B. Jets 2s, EMP-sized pull. Turn away or you go in. Castles / Hub / Station / Battleship immune.

ENEMIES

HUNTER

Dart. 2 HP (Easy). Score 75. Castles spawn them. Speed and fire rate ramp with the assault wave: by wave 15 they are 2× faster and shoot 3× as often. Twenty of them also escort the wave-4+ strike pack and stay on the formation’s flanks.

RAIDER

Attacks the Hub. 3 HP. Score 150. Count and rate come from the assault plan. Can ram the Hub. Wave 4+ also dumps a spread 20-raider strike pack every ~5 minutes; those ships do not count against the regular raider cap.

DESTROYER — from wave 4

48 hull. 100-hit shield (missiles do 10 shield each; guns often ricochet 42% / pierce 22%). Twin hold-lasers: 3s charge, 5s recharge, range 780. Slow (cap 110, turn 0.2). Score 250.

CORVETTE — from wave 9, one at a time

Mini-battleship hull. Attacks the Hub. Evade is first: if the player is close (860) or lined up, it banks away from the Hub and runs. Lined up → short warp (~440 over 0.2s) after it faces the cut, then 7.5s cool. Mid-warp: no shots, missiles, or ram.

CRANE

Unarmed yard tugs. 4 HP, slow. Waves 5–6 build the station; wave 9 the battleship. Score 100.

CASTLE STARS

Homing bolts from live castles. More on wave 2, storm on wave 8.

STATION — wave 5+

Ring 980, hub 220, 8 spokes. Guns 4 HP, modules 30, joints / clamps 80. Blow both edge joints and the spoke clamp to drop a section. Half the ring starts loose.

BATTLESHIP — wave 9+

~8× destroyer length. 6 plates, 2 start on. 10 ACRP rail shots to kill. Score 1200.

COMBAT RULES

LOCK / RUNWAY
Lock cam on a target. Corvettes always get their own runway strip.
AI TURRETS
Assault / Gunship / wingmen. Invisible fire. 3-hit packs vs hunters and raiders.
MISSILES
Drop, then ramp 520 → 1040. Fuse / blast. Ammo only.
ROCKETS
Gunship only. Shorter, amber, 2 damage.
EMP
Grow 0.72s, fade 0.28s, cool 8s. Tier 4 kills. Blinds missiles.
HOLE
Gated off. Flip blackHole.enabled to restore. B. Jet 2s, EMP-sized pull.
MAP
Hold to zoom out. Pause / end run are bound actions.
SAVE
V opens five slots. Cursor or 1–5 writes that slot with a ship snapshot. New Game does not erase slots. Title is NEW GAME / SLOT 1–5.

CONTROLS

Rebindable. Profiles: MOUSE / PAD / LAPTOP / PHONE.

Thrust, Reverse, Strafe L/R, Turn L/R, Aim, Fire, Shield, Warp, EMP, Missile, Map, Lock, Home, Pause, Save (V, pick slot 1–5), Fullscreen, End Run.

Phone has a d-pad + stick. Last direction latches if a finger slides off.
